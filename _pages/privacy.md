### Optional LinkedIn analytics

When LinkedIn analytics is enabled on this site, it loads only after you accept
the tracking notice. LinkedIn uses cookies and collects visit information such as
the page URL, referring page, IP address, browser and device characteristics, and
visit time. This helps Kevin understand the professional audience visiting the
site through aggregate reports such as job titles, companies, and industries.
These reports do not disclose individual visitors’ names to Kevin.

LinkedIn may also use this information for advertising measurement and
personalization under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy).
See [LinkedIn’s explanation of the Insight Tag](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7161779)
for its data handling and retention practices.

You can decline tracking and still use the website and chatbot. Use **Tracking
preferences** in the footer of a tracked page to change your choice. Withdrawing
consent stops further loading of the tag; it does not erase data already sent to
LinkedIn or necessarily remove existing LinkedIn cookies. You can also clear
cookies using your browser settings. The site honors Global Privacy Control by
keeping the tag disabled.

Your tracking choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If you clear
browser storage or use a different browser, the site asks again. No tracking tag
loads on this privacy page. This integration does not send chatbot message text
as custom analytics events.

### Chatbot conversations

The chatbot uses OpenAI to process messages. Kevin can review conversations in a
private database for 90 days after their last activity. Avoid sharing sensitive
information. Blocked or unchecked messages may be retained for private review.

The browser stores the displayed conversation, conversation ID, draft, and pending
request in tab-scoped session storage so you can continue when switching pages.
**New chat** resets the browser session; it does not delete the server’s records.
Closing the browser tab ends the browser session, subject to your browser’s session
restoration behavior.

Requests to OpenAI disable stored Responses API state. OpenAI’s applicable
abuse-monitoring retention still applies; see
[OpenAI’s data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).
